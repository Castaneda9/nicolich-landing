The problem we solve

Who it's for: leaders and owners who need not "more charts" but a control panel — COO / head of contact center or sales; CCO with one set of numbers; owners and CEOs; teams tired of endless reports and rework.

Typical picture:

  • lots of reports, but few decisions;
  • the same KPIs calculated differently in Excel, CRM and "that spreadsheet Maria keeps";
  • pretty dashboard, but in 30 seconds it's unclear: what broke and where to dig;
  • answering a simple question ("who is overloaded?", "where did we drop?") takes an analyst all day;
  • you spent time and money on dashboards, but work didn't get easier;

We design dashboards as a management tool, not a data showcase.

03

What makes a good dashboard

  1. The dashboard answers a specific question defined at design time — not "everything in the database".
  2. It shows the key metric.
  3. Metrics are connected to each other.
  4. The screen reads top to bottom: signal → cause → details.
  5. Leaders need a deviation signal, not a wall of numbers.
  6. Few key metrics on the first screen, each with a clear definition.
  7. Filters are aligned: change period or manager — everything recalculates.
  8. Deviation is visible immediately (vs prior period); "bad" metrics use inverted color.
  9. From a signal you can drill into details — not a dead-end chart.
  10. Rollout without a usage walkthrough is incomplete: show how to use it.

What we don't do

  • a chart for a single number;
  • a dashboard that needs ten minutes of explanation;
  • long dashboards with unrelated metrics;
  • a pie chart with a dozen segments "because it looks nice".

06

Dashboard types we build

Strategic

Goal achievement for a business unit or company — quarter and year horizon. For top management: the big picture without operational detail.

Question Are we hitting strategic margin goals and is the portfolio healthy overall?

On screen Plan vs actual margin (year/quarter), portfolio split (growing / stable / declining), product traffic light.

Decision Close a loss-making line, double down on a growing product, revisit pricing on a mass SKU with falling margin.

Analytical

Data from several operational sources, longer horizon, derived metrics: segments, shares, deviations — holistic view of product or direction.

Question At which lifecycle stage do we lose the most valuable customers and what triggers churn?

On screen Retention cohorts, churn map by plan/segment, churn reasons tied to last action, lost revenue.

Decision Proactive trigger before churn, tailored onboarding, win-back offer for top leavers.

Operational

Here and now: day, week. Tables and lists — a "bank statement" of activity.

Question Are we missing revenue plan in the last hour and which managers need a push?

On screen Revenue plan/fact now, leader/laggard funnel by check size, unpaid orders count.

Decision Call unpaid orders, route warm leads to strong reps, short discount for laggards.

09

Case: contact center workload

Production reference: analytics for open lines, calls and email — manager workload inside Bitrix24.

Goal: see customer communication load per manager.

What it reveals

  • how much communication flows through the team;
  • where the bottleneck is: missed, slow response, after-hours work;
  • which managers and channels carry volume;
  • peak days and hours;
  • response time per each conversation;
  • outbound vs inbound skew;
  • after-hours work as a process signal, not "another number";
  • foundation for linking load to conversion.

Manager decisions

  • reschedule shifts and standups away from peak hours;
  • hire additional staff;
  • reduce marketing activity;
  • investigate "missed" spikes by channel or agent;
  • spot outbound vs inbound imbalance;
  • catch after-hours work as a process signal.

On screen

  1. Filters: period, agents, channels (+ timezone context).
  2. KPI cards vs prior period.
  3. Trends, density heatmap, splits by manager and channel.
  4. Drilldown: conversation list with same filters, open each chat, email or call.
  5. Excel export, working hours setup, debug info for data quality checks.
